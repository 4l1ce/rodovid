# Worked example: source maps for Borzna (city) and Vybli (Chernihiv uezd)

Built Nov 2026 from the ДАЧО «Каталог метричних книг», ф. 805 опис and
FamilySearch catalogs 1348306, 1352403, 1355049. Use as a model for the
table a source map should end with, and as starting facts for these places.

## Borzna (city) — metric books, ДАЧО ф. 679

Churches: Благовіщенська, Василівська, Воскресенська (Вознесенська),
Різдва Христова, Миколаївська, Троїцька (+ Часноківка slobidka).

| Years | Справи | Online |
|---|---|---|
| 1805, 1806, 1824, 1827, 1834, 1836, 1839, 1842 | оп. 1 / 4 / 14, single volumes | FS 7781383 … 7781376 |
| **1843–1874** | **not preserved for any city church** | — |
| 1875–1884 | оп. 10 (not every church every year) | FS 7767340–7767363 |
| 1885–1918 | оп. 10, yearly (1891: only Часноківка) | FS 7767311 … 7776875 |

Confession registers (ф. 679): 1787, 1790, 1803, 1806, 1810 (Троїцька),
1811, 1813, 1824, 1826, 1846 — none after 1846. ДАЧО's own scans of
several are sharper than FamilySearch's (useful for water damage).

Revisions (ф. 805 / ф. 132): Cossacks of the city survive for **1850**
(805-367, film 7777455) and **1858** (805-405, city = арк. 701–882);
city Cossacks 1816 and 1834 are **not** in ДАЧО; 1834 805-390 = burghers,
merchants, Jews, colonists; 805-391 = landowners' peasants (incl.
Слобідка Часноківка арк. 348–352).

18th century: Rumyantsev Description 1767 (ЦДІАК ф. 57; FS DGS
109554258, draft and fair copy), confession register 1763 (ЦДІАК
127-1016-23). 1748/1755 registers (127-1015-10а, -24) cover only the
Maksakivsky monastery villages, not the city.

Civil registry ф. Р-8998 (FS 1362713): births 1915–1927 with gaps,
marriages 1926–1927, deaths 1919–1926; **no marriages 1921–1925**.

## Vybli (Chernihiv uezd) — what is online and what is not

- Metric books: FamilySearch collection «Украина, Чернигов, церковные
  записи» (partly indexed) — births 1870s–1910s, marriages, deaths
  (e.g. DGS 007767513, 007767783, 007783626, 007783330).
- Metric 1725–1740 fragments; Rumyantsev Description 1766 (two copies);
  1782 Cossack revision; confession registers only **1773 and 1812**.
- **No online revisions 1816, 1834, 1850, 1858** for Vybli Cossacks —
  both FS catalogs checked item by item; ф. 132 оп. 2 спр. 348
  (1795–1800, ~2000 unindexed images) not yet mapped.
- Landowners' peasants of the neighbouring village Підгірне: absent
  from the online 1782 revision (Cossacks only) → archive request.
- Next for the archive: volost family/conscription lists 1874–1900;
  ф. 185 оп. 2 спр. 6 (former serfs of landowner Markovych, 1875–76);
  tax books ф. 132 оп. 1 спр. 912, 949, 975.
