# Record types — what each one can prove

| Record | Years (Left-Bank) | What it names | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Метрична книга** (parish register): births/baptisms, marriages, deaths | ~1720s fragments; regular from 1780s; gaps common (e.g. Borzna city 1843–1874 lost) | Child, parents' given names + patronymic + surname, father's estate (козак, міщанин, селянин-власник, солдат), godparents; bride/groom with age, marital status, witnesses (поручителі); deceased with age and cause | Direct evidence of parent–child links and marriages. Godparents and witnesses reveal kin networks |
| **Сповідний розпис** (confession register) | 1740s–1900s, survival patchy | Household by household: head, wife, children, in-laws, ages, whether they confessed | Reconstructs households and ages between metric books; bridges gaps where metric books are lost |
| **Ревізька казка** (revision list) | 1782 (Cossacks), 1795, 1811, 1816, 1834, 1850, 1858 | Male souls (from 1795 also women) per household with ages in this and the previous revision, deaths and departures since then | Links generations across 20–40 years; "previous revision" age column bridges to older lists |
| **Румянцевський опис** (Rumyantsev Description) | 1765–1769 | Every household of a sotnia: head, family members, sometimes origin ("наслідний отцівський", "по жені"), property, subtenants | 18th-century anchor; connects to 1782 revision and 1763 confession registers |
| **Генеральне слідство про маєтності** | 1729–1731 | Estates and owners | Village history, not individual Cossacks |
| **Посімейні / призовні списки** (family and conscription lists) | 1874–1917 | Whole families incl. ages, by volost | Bridges 1858 → 1900s; often only in the archive, not online |
| **Перепис 1920** (1920 census cards) | 1920 | Household members, ages, occupation, literacy | Soviet-era household snapshot, household numbers (e.g. 823-г) show subdivided families |
| **РАЦС/ЗАГС** civil registers | 1919– | Births, marriages, deaths | Post-revolution events; access to records < 75/100 years may be restricted |
| **Military records** (WWI, WWII) | 1914–1918, 1941–1945 | Name, patronymic, birth year and place, unit, fate | Confirms patronymics and birthplaces; links emigrant / deceased branches |

## Estates (стани) and why they matter

- **Козаки** (Left-Bank, after 1783 "малоросійські козаки"): taxable; in
  revision lists of Cossacks; separate from peasants.
- **Посполиті / поміщицькі селяни**: serfs of a landowner, listed under
  the owner's name in revisions; after 1861 "селяни-власники".
- **Міщани / купці**: urban estates, listed in city revisions.
- **Солдати, відставні нижні чини**: recruits leave their household;
  retired soldiers listed separately (1858 lists of retired soldiers).
- **Почесні громадяни, дворяни, духовенство**: separate records.
A change in estate (козак → міщанин → почесний громадянин) explains
why a family "disappears" from one record type and appears in another.

## Absence of evidence

Absence in a record answers the question only if that record is
complete for the place and period (check coverage, lost leaves,
illegible scans, unindexed films). Always write where you looked.
